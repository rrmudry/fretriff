⚡ The Beginner Electric Guitar Playbook

1. What is the "Easy Dozen" for Guitar?

In ukulele, the "Easy Dozen" teaches open chords to play thousands of songs. On guitar, the foundation is:

  • The 8 Open Foundation Chords ("The Big 8"): E Major, E Minor, A Major, A Minor, D Major, D Minor, C Major, and G Major.
  • The 4 Essential Dominant 7ths: E7, A7, D7, and B7 (the heartbeat of blues and classic rock).

2. The Electric Guitar Secret Weapon: Power Chords (5 Chords)

Unlike acoustic or ukulele, electric guitar has Power Chords (E5, A5, D5, G5, C5, F5). They only contain the Root and 5th note (no major/minor third), which means:

  • You only need 2 fingers!
  • They NEVER clash with distortion or overdrive.
  • They are movable shapes: slide the same shape up and down the neck to play Green Day, AC/DC, Nirvana, and Foo Fighters instantly.

3. Why Chords Sound Good Together (The Roman Numeral Secret)

Chords work together like a story:

  • I (The Tonic / "Home"): The starting ground (e.g. G in Key of G). Feels complete and safe.
  • IV (The Subdominant / "The Journey"): Lifts energy and moves away from home (e.g. C).
  • V (The Dominant / "The Tension"): Creates a strong magnetic pull that wants to snap back Home (e.g. D).
  • vi (The Minor / "The Emotion"): Adds mystery, moodiness, and soul (e.g. Em).
